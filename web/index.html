SCIENTIFIC RECORD

Methods & provenance

Transparent assumptions and protocol constraints for credible computational research.

Primary measurement processing

Experimental endpoints are not pooled. Exact positive nM values are converted to negative-log molar activity. Structure parsing and parent standardization use RDKit. Within-assay duplicates and conflicting measurements are audited.

ChEMBL selection additionally requires human single-protein target assignment, confidence score 9, binding assay context, standardization and source documentation. Exclusions are retained in a downloadable manifest.

Data-source comparability

ChEMBL exposes assay-level metadata; BindingDB REST results have limited experimental metadata and affinity-cutoff selection. PubChem assay downloads may mix binary outcomes and endpoint-specific readouts.

These sources must not be pooled without record-level normalization, source overlap detection, assay compatibility verification and independent provenance checks.

Repeated grouped cross-validation

Outer grouped folds are defined using molecular scaffolds, molecular structures, or genuine assay IDs. For every outer fold, an independent group-held-out calibration subset is reserved from the training partition.

Assay metadata encoders fit on training observations only. Sample weights for the quality ablation are computed inside each fitting fold. No target labels from the held-out test fold enter fitting or calibration.

Uncertainty & reporting

Conformal intervals use an absolute-residual finite-sample quantile. Marginal coverage requires exchangeability, which scaffold and assay shifts can violate. The reported empirical coverage is descriptive rather than guaranteed.

Reports include per-fold and seed-level scores, out-of-fold predictions, configuration, software versions, dataset checksums and explicit limitations. Model search or statistical superiority claims require independent validation.
